04 / MODEL BASIS

SCIENCE &
ASSUMPTIONS.

Transparent calculations and limitations are essential. This website is a learning aid, not a validated recovery system.

01

Incoming particulate mass

mPM = Q × CPM × t

Flow rate, particulate concentration and duration determine the illustrative mass entering the system. Units must be converted consistently.

02

Captured mass

mcaptured = mPM × η

Capture efficiency (η) is an assumed input. Real efficiency depends on filter design, particle size and operating conditions.

03

Recovered fraction

mrecovered = mcaptured × R

Recovery (R) is an assumed fraction retained after treatment, not a measured carbon purity value.

04

Estimated ink mass

mink = mrecovered ÷ φ

The solids fraction (φ) gives an illustrative formulation estimate; it does not establish ink quality or print performance.

!

Important limitations

  • Vehicle exhaust contains gases and a complex mix of particulate species.
  • Particulate matter must not be treated as pure carbon without laboratory characterization.
  • Real collection and purification require suitable equipment, risk assessment and emissions controls.
  • Sustainability, yield and ink-quality claims need experimental evidence.
